## Area

Discovery and the public calendar cover Markham, Scarborough, Richmond Hill, Thornhill, Stouffville, Toronto/North York, Vaughan and Mississauga. Woodbridge, Concord and Maple are grouped under Vaughan; Etobicoke and East York under Toronto. These are city boundaries, not measured driving distances. Mississauga and Vaughan are the outer western/northern destinations; Ajax, Newmarket, Brampton, Oakville and farther cities are excluded.

The former Brampton CardFesta record remains available in existing installations through Catalogue and any saved plans, but is no longer in discovery or the public calendar. Saved-only calendars preserve such personal plans.

## Review method and qualifications

Review the organizer's website, Eventbrite listing and OnTreasure event/table pages. Search-engine snippets and old organizer homepages can lag behind newly published events. Open the rendered detail pages and compare actual dates and venues, not the URL slug. Inspect full-resolution schedule posters as well as page text: image-only announcements are valid sources. Include announced dates when the city is within scope, marking an unpublished venue explicitly and withholding directions. Separate occurrences by date and booking page; do not duplicate the same occurrence across platforms.

- **HobbyCon:** visually reviewed the [2026 annual schedule poster](https://assets.zyrosite.com/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,w=1024,h=1024,fit=crop/mjE7OXWJovtG6qa9/copy-of-copy-of-talking-vendor-application-6-iT1njszpujhOtj6T.jpg) and [Markham night-market poster](https://assets.zyrosite.com/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,w=1024,h=1024,fit=crop/mjE7OXWJovtG6qa9/6ee637da-4570-4864-b6dc-94b9b28c2bc7-y45JZnqgDVX5sFLr.jpeg) linked from the official schedule page. Added eight daytime dates at Eros (Mississauga), Brighton (Scarborough), New Jaasmin (Markham) and Venu (Vaughan), plus October 29, November 5 and December 17 Markham night markets, 4–9 pm. The night-market year is inferred from the surrounding 2026 schedule; its poster omits the year. Its venue is unannounced: do not reuse New Jaasmin from the separate daytime series. Brighton supersedes the directory's outdated White Shield venue. Free entry is published; table fees, availability, booking opening date and historic sales/attendance are unknown. The [vendor application](https://hobbyconevents.com/vendor-application) takes requests for desired dates and is not proof of table availability. Past dates and shows outside the local area are excluded.
- **HobbyVerse:** Eventbrite confirms November 28–29 at WePlay, 100 Clegg Road. OnTreasure sells Saturday and Sunday tables separately, so the app has one entry per day. Both table maps have available standard 6-foot tables priced C$125 in their descriptions, despite the system's Free label. The opening date of table sales is not published. Saturday's event header and Eventbrite search list 11 am; the OnTreasure description says 10 am. The app uses 11 am with the discrepancy noted. Sunday agrees on 11 am–5 pm.
- **Mississauga Card Show:** the current November listing shows **November 15**, although its URL and old search snippets say November 1. March's occurrence uses a different address, 4300 Cawthra Road. Do not reuse November's venue or prices.
- **Collectors Clash:** use the poster/organizer description's public hours of 10:30 am–7 pm; its header starts at 9 am.
- **Greater Toronto Holiday Expo:** Eventbrite resolves the previous conflicting website dates to **December 27–28** at Montecassino, North York. The continuous event range confirms Sunday opens at 10 am and Monday closes at 5 pm; Sunday closing and Monday opening remain TBA.

- OnTreasure vendor counts and Eventbrite lifetime organizer attendance are not event attendance forecasts. No popularity rating or booking opening date was inferred.
- Other advertised table prices are starting prices, not an assertion of current stock or total checkout cost. Table status remains Unknown unless availability was inspected. Prices and availability are snapshots.

- Mixed collectible conventions are labeled as such. Weekly store tournaments and small trade nights are excluded.

## Updating

This is a reviewed bundled snapshot, not an automatic web scraper. The Discover footer opens [OnTreasure Ontario](https://www.ontreasure.com/events?states=ON) and [Eventbrite Markham card shows](https://www.eventbrite.ca/d/canada--markham/card-show/) for current source discovery. External results can include cities outside the app's area.

Existing installations receive missing IDs once per catalogue revision. Private plans, edited listings and archive choices are preserved. Updates fill missing daily hours only when date, city and venue still match and no hours have been entered; edited schedules are preserved. Other existing fields are not overwritten; use a reviewed Catalogue import for corrections to existing IDs. Failed writes roll back.

Unknown amounts use -1; free general admission uses 0. A booking link alone does not prove availability. Check organizer details before travel or payment. Synthetic example feeds are never loaded automatically.
