# Event sources

Catalogue checked **November 10, 2026**. **72 upcoming entries** across the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area, including HobbyCon dates read from official posters and entries reviewed through OnTreasure, Eventbrite and organizer websites. The December 17 Markham night market has an announced date and city but no venue yet; the October 29 and November 5 ones are confirmed at New Jaasmin. HobbyVerse has separate Saturday/Sunday table bookings. This is a reviewed snapshot, not an exhaustive live service.

## Area

Discovery and the public calendar cover the whole Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area (since October 24, 2026; before that only Markham, Scarborough, Richmond Hill, Thornhill, Stouffville, Toronto/North York, Vaughan and Mississauga, which is why shows such as HobbyCon Ajax were left out). Regions: Toronto, York Region, Durham, Peel, Halton and Hamilton; see README.md for the city names and the towns grouped under each. Barrie, Kitchener-Waterloo, Guelph, Niagara and farther cities are excluded.

Brampton is inside the area again since October 24, 2026, so the older Brampton CardFesta record and the new CardFesta Winter Expo appear in discovery and the public calendar. Saved-only calendars still preserve personal plans for shows outside the area.

## Review method and qualifications

Review the organizer's website, Eventbrite listing and OnTreasure event/table pages. Search-engine snippets and old organizer homepages can lag behind newly published events. Open the rendered detail pages and compare actual dates and venues, not the URL slug. Inspect full-resolution schedule posters as well as page text: image-only announcements are valid sources. Include announced dates when the city is within scope, marking an unpublished venue explicitly and withholding directions. Separate occurrences by date and booking page; do not duplicate the same occurrence across platforms.

## Updating

This is a reviewed bundled snapshot, not an automatic web scraper. The Discover footer opens [OnTreasure Ontario](https://www.ontreasure.com/events?states=ON) and [Eventbrite Markham card shows](https://www.eventbrite.ca/d/canada--markham/card-show/) for current source discovery. External results can include cities outside the app's area.

Existing installations receive missing IDs once per catalogue revision. Private plans, edited listings and archive choices are preserved. Updates fill missing daily hours only when date, city and venue still match and no hours have been entered; edited schedules are preserved. Other existing fields are not overwritten; use a reviewed Catalogue import for corrections to existing IDs. Failed writes roll back.

Unknown amounts use -1; free general admission uses 0. A booking link alone does not prove availability. Check organizer details before travel or payment. Synthetic example feeds are never loaded automatically.
